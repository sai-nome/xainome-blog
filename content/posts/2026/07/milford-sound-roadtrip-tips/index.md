## 日本語版

### Te Anau

今回はMilford Soundに行ってきたのでそのことについて書いていこうと思います。今回は車中泊じゃなくTe Anauという場所に宿を取りました。車でQueenstownから出発すると時間がかかりますし、バスでもかなり時間がかかるので。

今回宿泊した場所はここになります。見た目は良いですし、ベッドや設備もよいのでかなり快適に過ごせました。ここにはキングベッドが一つ、2段ベッドでクイーンとシングルのセットでした。バスルームは1つですね。

### Milford Sound　道中

ここから大体車で2時間ほど走らせるとMilford Soundに着きます。道中は少し危ないので速度を抑えつつ運転するのをお勧めします。

港に着いたら車をパーキングエリアに停めて向かいます。パーキングエリアは有料しかないので大体$40くらい支払う必要があります。他のとこよりも少し高めですね。

私はすでにオンラインで予約して決済も済ませていたので受付でチケットをもらうだけでした。チケットをもらったら所定の船に向かって乗船するだけですね。

行きの方向では晴れていました。とても綺麗で風も出ていたので心地よかったです。各岬の崖や周りの森も神秘的でたまに見えた滝もよかったですね。

滝がある場所では船が少しの間止まって写真を撮るタイミングを作ってくれました。私の場合は2か所くらいですね。高い場所から落ちてくる滝を見るのはとても壮大でした。

### Milford Sound 注意点

一通りぐるっと周って帰ってきて終わりですね。ここからが私の注意点になりますが、一番は天気が重要になります。旅行をするうえで天気は重要ですが、ここはかなり大事ですね。というのも私が行ったときは水量がかなり少なかったのです。

天気としては1週間くらいずっと晴れが続いている状況でした。なので水が蒸発して動画などで見る水量よりもかなり少ない状況でした。Milford Soundは7割以上が雨になるらしいですが私の時は腫れでしたし。

というわけでもしMilford Soundをたっぷり楽しみたい場合は、事前に雨がしっかり降っている状況でその日は比較的晴れている日を選ぶのが一番賢いのだと思います。行く前はしっかり調べておくと私のようにがっかりすることはなくなるかと思います。ではでは。

## 重要な全体ミス

### 1\. 「accommodation」は少し注意

× I stayed this accommodation.  
○ I stayed **at** this accommodation.  
○ I stayed **at this place**.

**stay + at 場所** が基本です。

* * *

### 2\. “take” の使い方

× it took for a long time  
○ it **takes a long time**  
○ it **would take a long time**

“take for a long time” は不可です。

* * *

### 3\. 冠詞 a / the がかなり抜けています

× I parked in parking area  
○ I parked in **a** parking area  
○ I parked in **the** parking area

× choose sunny date  
○ choose **a** sunny day

IELTSでは冠詞ミスが多いと Grammar のスコアが上がりにくいです。

* * *

### 4\. 単数・複数のミス

× facility  
○ facilities

× amount of water were  
○ the amount of water **was**

“amount” は単数なので **was** です。

* * *

### 5\. スペルミスが多い

| 誤り | 正しい形 |
| --- | --- |
| wtih | with |
| purchaced | purchased |
| clue staff | crew staff / crew |
| clusing | cruise / closing ※文脈では cruise |
| wether | weather |
| disapointed | disappointed |

スペルミスは Lexical Resource でかなり減点されます。

## 文ごとの細かい修正

### 1

× I am going to write about Milford Sound.  
△ 意味は通じますが、少し不自然です。

○ I’m going to write about **my trip to Milford Sound**.

「Milford Soundについて」より「Milford Soundに行った旅について」のほうが自然です。

* * *

### 2

× I booked accommodation in Te Anau because it took for a long time to go there from Queenstown by bus even though bus.

問題点：  
“it took for a long time” が文法ミスです。  
“even though bus” は意味が壊れています。  
日本語では「車でもバスでも時間がかかる」と言っています。

○ I booked accommodation in Te Anau because it would take a long time to get to Milford Sound from Queenstown, whether by car or by bus.

* * *

### 3

× I stayed this accommodation.

○ I stayed at this accommodation.  
○ This is the place where I stayed.

* * *

### 4

× It looked good and I felt comfortable because of beds and facility.

問題点：  
“beds and facility” が不自然です。  
facility は複数なら facilities。  
“because of beds” も少し不自然です。

○ It looked nice, and I felt very comfortable because the beds and facilities were good.

* * *

### 5

× There were a king bed and double bed wtih queen and single.

問題点：  
“were a king bed” は不自然。  
“with queen and single” だけでは意味が不足。  
“wtih” はスペルミス。

○ There was one king bed and a bunk bed with a queen-sized bed and a single bed.

* * *

### 6

× Moreover, there was a bathroom.

文法的には大きく間違いではありませんが、少し硬いです。

○ There was also one bathroom.

### 7

× Milford Sound on the way

○ On the way to Milford Sound  
○ The drive to Milford Sound

### 8

× I arrived at Milford Sound after driving for 2 hours.

○ I arrived at Milford Sound after driving for about two hours.

“about” を入れると「大体2時間」の意味になります。

× I recommend driving it speeds a little.

これはかなり不自然です。

○ I recommend driving carefully and keeping your speed down.

「速度を抑える」は **keep your speed down** が自然です。

“in parking area” → a/the が必要  
“needed paying” → needed to pay  
“arriving the harbor” → arriving at the harbour  
文が長くて構造が崩れています。

○ After arriving at the harbour, I parked in a paid parking area, which cost about $40.

* * *

### 11

× It was a little expensive.

○ It was a little pricey compared with other places.

“pricey” は「少し高め」という自然な表現です。

### 12

× I booked and purchaced online so I received a ticket at the reception before I rode on the ferry.

問題点：  
purchaced → purchased  
“booked and paid online” のほうが自然  
“rode on the ferry” より “boarded the boat/cruise”

○ I had already booked and paid online, so I only had to pick up my ticket at reception before boarding the boat.

### 13

× It was sunny and I felt comfortable because the view was so beautiful and it was good wind.

問題点：  
“it was good wind” は不自然。  
“there was a nice breeze” が自然です。

○ It was sunny on the way there, and it felt very pleasant because the views were beautiful and there was a nice breeze.

### 14

× In addition, I watched falls and gorgeous forest near capes.

問題点：  
watch は「じっと見る」感じ。景色には see / enjoy が自然。  
falls より waterfalls。  
capes は文脈的にやや不自然で、cliffs のほうがよいです。

○ The cliffs, surrounding forests, and occasional waterfalls looked almost mystical.

問題点：  
clue staff → crew  
made time は不自然

○ The crew stopped the boat briefly near the waterfalls so that passengers could take photos.

問題点：  
two points が不自然  
which falls were は文法ミス

○ This happened at about two spots, and the waterfalls dropping from high above were truly impressive.

× The clusing was finished after going around.

意味がかなり伝わりにくいです。  
“clusing” は単語として不自然です。文脈では cruise です。

○ The cruise ended after making a loop around the sound.

### 19

× The wether was most important because amount of water were a little.

問題点：  
wether → weather  
the most important が必要  
amount of water was  
“a little” ではなく “low”

○ The most important thing to consider is the weather, because the amount of water can be quite low.

### 20

× The wether kept sunny for a week so that amount of water were less than videos.

問題点：  
kept sunny → had been sunny  
so that はここでは不自然  
amount was  
less than videos → less than in the videos

○ It had been sunny for about a week, so there was much less water than I had seen in videos.

### 21

× It was sunny when I was in Milford Sound.

○ Although Milford Sound is said to be rainy most of the time, it was sunny when I visited.

### 22

× If you want to enjoy Milford Sound, you should check the wether and choose sunny date.

問題点：  
weather のスペル  
choose a sunny date → choose a sunny day  
ただし日本語では「雨の後の晴れの日」がベストと言っています。

○ If you want to fully enjoy Milford Sound, I think the best option is to go on a relatively sunny day after there has been plenty of rain.

* * *

### 23

× Therefore, you will not be disapointed like me.

問題点：  
Therefore が硬すぎる  
disapointed → disappointed

○ That way, you will be less likely to feel disappointed like I did.

Te Anau

This time, I’m going to write about my trip to Milford Sound. Instead of sleeping in my car, I booked accommodation in Te Anau, because getting to Milford Sound from Queenstown takes quite a long time, whether by car or by bus.

The place I stayed at was very comfortable. It looked nice, and both the beds and the facilities were good. There was one king bed, as well as a bunk bed with a queen-sized bed and a single bed. There was also one bathroom.

The drive to Milford Sound

From Te Anau, it takes about two hours by car to reach Milford Sound. Some parts of the road can be a little dangerous, so I recommend driving carefully and keeping your speed down.

After arriving at the harbour, I parked in a paid parking area and walked to the terminal. There did not seem to be any free parking nearby, so I had to pay about $40, which felt a little expensive compared with other places.

I had already booked and paid online, so all I had to do was pick up my ticket at reception. After that, I simply went to the designated boat and boarded it.

On the way out, the weather was sunny. The scenery was beautiful, and there was a pleasant breeze, so it felt very comfortable. The cliffs, surrounding forests, and occasional waterfalls created a mysterious and impressive atmosphere.

When the boat approached the waterfalls, the crew stopped for a short time so that passengers could take photos. This happened at about two spots. Seeing waterfalls drop from such a great height was truly spectacular.

Things to keep in mind about Milford Sound

After the boat made a loop around the sound, the cruise came to an end. From my experience, the most important thing to consider when visiting Milford Sound is the weather. Of course, weather matters whenever you travel, but I think it is especially important here. When I visited, the amount of water was much lower than I had expected.

It had been sunny for about a week, so there was far less water than I had seen in videos. I have heard that Milford Sound is rainy more than 70 percent of the time, but when I went, the weather was clear.

So, if you want to fully enjoy Milford Sound, I think the best option is to visit on a relatively sunny day after there has been plenty of rain. If you check the conditions carefully before going, you will be less likely to feel disappointed like I did. See you next time.
